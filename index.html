The title of the project: Homelessness Project

My name: Pengfang Chen

Project Description: In this project, I will visualize the US homeless people distribution data. It includes five types of charts. A proportional symbol map will display each state location and amounts. Pie charts will display each state's categorical views. A horizontal bar chart will compare the states amounts and helps us to see which is the largest number. A line chart can help us know about the time trend change. Finally a parallel coordinate plot will display multiple variables (such as state, total homeless population, sheltered, unsheltered, chronic, and family categories) simultaneously. Through these visualizations, users will gain a comprehensive understanding of homelessness distribution and patterns across U.S. states.
